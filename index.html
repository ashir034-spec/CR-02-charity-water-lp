What is charity: water?

charity: water is a nonprofit organization focused on bringing clean, safe drinking water to people in need around the world. By funding wells, taps, and other community-led water solutions, they help unlock better health, more time for school and work, and stronger local economies.

How your gift is put to work

Donations support proven water projects delivered with trusted local partners. From planning and construction to training and long-term maintenance, the goal is reliable access that communities can sustain.

Track your impact

After you give, you’ll receive project updates so you can follow along — including where funds are headed, progress milestones, and stories from the field. Many projects share location details, photos, and completion reports, so you can see the impact you helped create.
